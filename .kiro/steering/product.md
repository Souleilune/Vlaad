# Product: Project AGOS-BD

**Adaptive Geo-mapped Outreach System for Blood Donations**

A full-stack emergency-first web platform for community blood availability reporting, live emergency requests, trusted-source verification, and donation tracking in the Philippines.

## Core Features

- **Public map** — guests can browse active blood reports and emergency requests without an account
- **Blood reports** — community members submit requests or donor offers with location, blood type, and contact info
- **Emergency requests** — urgent hospital/patient needs with urgency levels and status tracking
- **Verified sources** — hospitals, Red Cross branches, LGUs, and donation centers get a verified badge
- **Donor dashboard** — registered users track donation streaks, reputation score, badges, and next eligible date
- **Admin panel** — moderation queue, user role management, announcements, and analytics
- **Realtime updates** — Supabase Realtime pushes map and feed changes to all connected clients

## User Roles

- `guest` — read-only public map and announcements
- `user` — submit reports, view dashboard, receive notifications
- `admin` — full moderation, analytics, and user management

## Design Identity

Warm emergency-response palette: deep crimson, warm coral, clean white, soft gold. Nunito as the single typeface for display and body copy.
